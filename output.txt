2.2 Factorial Interaction Discrepancy Resolution & Arithmetic Reconciliation:
- Problem Investigated:
  Previous draft report showed deltas (+150k BDT, +40k BDT, +265k BDT) and stated synergy was +5,000 BDT.
  However, 265k - (150k + 40k) = 75,000 BDT. The discrepancy was investigated and resolved:
  * The +5,000 BDT originated from evaluating perturbations from the field's normal profile
    (180 mm irrig, 110 kg N) rather than the Low/Low control treatment (108 mm irrig, 66 kg N).
- Implementation Resolution: Both mathematical formulations are now explicitly computed and exposed:
  * Formulation A (Classic 2x2 ANOVA Synergy with Low/Low as reference):
    Synergy_2x2 = P(1,1) - P(1,0) - P(0,1) + P(0,0)
    For Rajshahi Barind Tract (8.0 ha, Rice):
      P(0,0) [Low Irrig: 108 mm, Low N: 66 kg]:   364,276 BDT (Yield: 3.21 t/ha)
      P(1,0) [High Irrig: 234 mm, Low N: 66 kg]:  478,472 BDT (Yield: 3.82 t/ha | Delta: +114,196 BDT)
      P(0,1) [Low Irrig: 108 mm, High N: 143 kg]: 530,652 BDT (Yield: 3.91 t/ha | Delta: +166,376 BDT)
      P(1,1) [High Irrig: 234 mm, High N: 143 kg]: 679,848 BDT (Yield: 4.66 t/ha | Delta: +315,572 BDT)
      Classic 2x2 Synergy = 315,572 - (114,196 + 166,376) = +35,000 BDT (Yield: +0.14 t/ha).
  * Formulation B (Baseline-Centered Perturbation with Normal Profile as reference):
    Synergy_base = Delta(Both High) - [Delta(High Irrig Alone) + Delta(High N Alone)]
    For Rajshahi Normal Profile (180 mm irrig, 110 kg N):
      Normal Profit: 599,960 BDT (Yield: 4.26 t/ha)
      High Irrig Alone (234 mm, 110 kg): Delta = +6,084 BDT (Yield: +0.09 t/ha)
      High N Alone (180 mm, 143 kg):     Delta = +68,804 BDT (Yield: +0.29 t/ha)
      Both High (234 mm, 143 kg):        Delta = +79,888 BDT (Yield: +0.40 t/ha)
      Baseline Synergy = 79,888 - (6,084 + 68,804) = +5,000 BDT (Yield: +0.02 t/ha).
  * Dynamic regression test `test_factorial_interaction_mathematical_consistency` asserts both
    formulations across all 6 profiles without hardcoded constants.
