2. SCIENTIFIC INVESTIGATION: RESOLUTION OF FACTORIAL INTERACTION DISCREPANCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Investigation of the 75,000 BDT vs 5,000 BDT Discrepancy:
- The previous draft report contained a stylized table presenting profit changes:
  * High irrigation, low nitrogen:  +150,000 BDT
  * Low irrigation, high nitrogen:   +40,000 BDT
  * High irrigation, high nitrogen: +265,000 BDT
  * Reported Synergy:                +5,000 BDT [labeled: 265k - (150k + 40k)]
- Root Cause Identified:
  * Arithmetic Inconsistency: 265,000 - (150,000 + 40,000) = 75,000 BDT, NOT 5,000 BDT.
  * Inconsistent Reference Baselines: The "+5,000 BDT" figure originated from the actual
    simulation output of `rajshahi_stressed` evaluated as a 3-point perturbation from the
    field's normal profile (180 mm irrig, 110 kg N), whereas the stylized table used the
    Low/Low treatment (108 mm irrig, 66 kg N) as baseline (0,0).

2.2 Mathematical Reconciliation of Both Factorial Formulations:
- Formulation A: Classic 2x2 Factorial ANOVA Synergy (Control = Low/Low):
  Synergy_2x2 = P(1,1) - P(1,0) - P(0,1) + P(0,0)
  For Rajshahi Barind Tract (8.0 ha, Rice):
  * P(0,0) [Low Irrig: 108 mm, Low N: 66 kg]:   364,276 BDT (Yield: 3.21 t/ha)
  * P(1,0) [High Irrig: 234 mm, Low N: 66 kg]:  478,472 BDT (Yield: 3.82 t/ha | Delta: +114,196 BDT)
  * P(0,1) [Low Irrig: 108 mm, High N: 143 kg]: 530,652 BDT (Yield: 3.91 t/ha | Delta: +166,376 BDT)
  * P(1,1) [High Irrig: 234 mm, High N: 143 kg]: 679,848 BDT (Yield: 4.66 t/ha | Delta: +315,572 BDT)
  Classic 2x2 Synergy = 315,572 - (114,196 + 166,376) = +35,000 BDT (Yield Synergy: +0.14 t/ha).

- Formulation B: Baseline-Centered Perturbation Synergy (Reference = Normal Field Profile):
  Synergy_base = Delta(Both High) - [Delta(High Irrig Alone) + Delta(High N Alone)]
  For Rajshahi Normal Baseline (180 mm irrig, 110 kg N):
  * Baseline Profit: 599,960 BDT (Yield: 4.26 t/ha)
  * High Irrig Alone (234 mm, 110 kg): Delta = +6,084 BDT (Yield: +0.09 t/ha)
  * High N Alone (180 mm, 143 kg):     Delta = +68,804 BDT (Yield: +0.29 t/ha)
  * Both High (234 mm, 143 kg):        Delta = +79,888 BDT (Yield: +0.40 t/ha)
  Baseline-Centered Synergy = 79,888 - (6,084 + 68,804) = +5,000 BDT (Yield Synergy: +0.02 t/ha).
